## Proven results for Louisiana students Proven results for Louisiana students

Independent research from the Center for Research & Reform in Education at Johns Hopkins University shows students in Louisiana districts that used Zearn Math with dedicated implementation support, like school coaching, educator training, and data monitoring, significantly outperformed peers on LEAP — with strongest gains for students who completed at least 90 grade-level digital lessons over the course of the school year.
