# Zearn Math in Ohio

Zearn Math is a top-rated math learning platform that gives Ohio students more opportunity to learn grade-level math with built-in, differentiated support.

Zearn Math is currently being offered to Ohio schools as part of a statewide initiative to accelerate math learning. Learn more about funding for 2025-26.

## Get to know Zearn Math

## Zearn Math Fact Sheet

A limited number of districts and schools will receive free access to Zearn Math for the 2025-26 school year. Learn more about Zearn Math in Ohio.

## How Zearn Works

Zearn Math is made by teachers to reinforce and deepen teacher-led instruction. Our math lessons are grounded in the leading research on math learning to give students more opportunities to explore, persevere and break through in the grade-level concepts they are learning in class.

## Instructional Use Case Overview

Zearn Math can support math learning during core math instruction, intervention time, tutoring and after-school programming.
