Zearn Math is an adaptive K-8 learning platform that guides students through daily opportunities to explore, persevere and break through in grade-level learning — with digital lessons rated “Strong” by Evidence for ESSA.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Zearn Math and how does it work?

Zearn Math is a nonprofit, online math learning program designed to help all students access and engage in grade-level math. Used as a supplement to daily math instruction, Zearn Math includes daily, adaptive digital math lessons for grades K-8. Each lesson includes activities to build procedural fluency, guided video lessons that introduce math concepts through a Concrete-to-Pictorial-to-Abstract (CPA) progression, and a closing mastery-based quiz. Throughout students’ learning, lessons adapt in real time to provide personalized feedback and scaffolding— supporting students through challenges while keeping them engaged in grade-level content.

### What grades does Zearn Math cover?

Zearn Math includes more than 1,000 digital lessons for every major math concept of grades K-8.

### Is Zearn free for teachers and students?

As a nonprofit, Zearn provides teachers with free access to our adaptive digital lessons for one classroom of up to 35 students. Schools and districts can purchase paid accounts that offer premium features and content to support implementation of Zearn at scale.

### What’s included in each Zearn digital lesson?

Every Zearn digital lesson follows a consistent structure: activities to build procedural fluency, an interactive video lesson to learn and reinforce new concepts, and a mastery-based quiz where students show understanding. Lessons also include guided prompts for paper-and-pencil problem solving to help students strengthen and retain new learning.

### Does Zearn Math work?

Zearn Math earned a Tier 1 “Strong” rating from Evidence for ESSA — the highest designation from a nationally recognized, independent clearinghouse that evaluates education research to help schools, districts, and states select evidence-based programs. This top rating confirms that Zearn Math meets the rigorous evidence standards outlined in the Every Student Succeeds Act (ESSA). ‍ This rating is based on a recent large-scale randomized controlled trial conducted from 2022–2024 with 10,000 students in grades 3–5 across 64 schools in a large urban Texas district. The study found causal evidence of positive, statistically significant impacts of Zearn on student math achievement, as measured by the nationally normed NWEA MAP Growth assessment. ‍ This study is part of a growing base of third-party evidence of Zearn’s impact, including a quasi-experimental evaluation in Louisiana by the Center for Research and Reform in Education (CRRE) at Johns Hopkins University, which found that students using Zearn with implementation support, such as school coaching, educator training, and usage monitoring, significantly outperformed peers on the state’s LEAP math assessment, with statistically significant gains across all student subgroups.

### Is Zearn aligned to state math standards?

Zearn is aligned to most state math standards, including full alignment to the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS). The platform is also customized in partnership with state education agencies.

### How can schools and teachers use Zearn to support math learning?

Zearn is designed to be used flexibly across instructional times to accelerate math learning. This can be during core math instruction as a Tier 1 digital support, during high-dosage tutoring, or in other windows like intervention or WIN time or after-school programming. Our entire content library is also available to families to access from home, 24/7.

### Can Zearn be used at home?

Families can sign up for free access to Zearn for use at home. Lessons are student-paced and include video instruction, interactive practice, and built-in support.

### Does Zearn offer resources for summer math learning?

Zearn offers a targeted summer math content series for rising 1st- through 9th-graders that covers key math concepts and helps set students up for grade-level learning in the fall. Each grade-level series is offered at no cost to students and teachers and includes digital math lessons that kids complete independently as well as instructional resources for educators. Lessons can be used flexibly across summer learning models, including in-school summer programming, tutoring or at home.
