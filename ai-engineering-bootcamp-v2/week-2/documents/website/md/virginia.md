# High-dosage tutoring with Zearn

Zearn’s K-8 math platform supports Virginia educators to accelerate math learning for all students.

The Virginia Department of Education has extended free access to Zearn for all divisions in the Commonwealth through 2025-26.

Watch: Accelerating middle school math learning with Zearn Math

In a recent webinar, Virginia educators joined the Zearn team to share their firsthand experiences implementing Zearn Math in grades 6-8.

## Get to know Zearn Math

## How Zearn Works

Zearn Math is made by teachers to reinforce and deepen teacher-led instruction. Our math lessons are grounded in the leading research on math learning to give students more opportunities to explore, persevere and break through in the grade-level concepts they are learning in class.

## Tutoring Guides

Zearn’s tutor resources support educators to flexibly implement personalized tutoring sessions. Explore grade-level tutoring guides.

## Math Motivation Hub

## Let’s make this summer count

Explore targeted math content designed to help Virginia students catch up and move forward in math learning this summer. Each 4-to-6-week series is designed to flexibly support summer math learning during in-school programming, tutoring or at home. Support summer math learning with the Brainy Challenge tracker—a fun way to motivate students to complete digital lessons.
