# Zearn Math Resources

## Need a little bit more info?

Here’s where we keep tons of helpful things you might need to support students’ math learning. From family-friendly materials to standards alignment guides and on-demand webinars, explore resources that will help you make the most of Zearn Math. Have a look around!

## Navigation guide

Download this step-by-step guide to navigating Zearn's digital math platform.

## Product list

Zearn is free for all individual teachers and classrooms—always. For schools and districts, we offer paid accounts with extra features as well as printed materials.

## Professional development

From dedicated professional development courses to on-demand curriculum study, Zearn Math PD helps educators make the most of Zearn. Discover professional learning sessions that will help your school or district make the most of Zearn Math.

## Webinar library

Recordings of webinars to discover more about Zearn Math and our approach. Webinars cover topics like our approach to assessments, supporting English language learners, etc.

## Materials & manipulatives

Looking for printed materials or manipulatives? Find out more about purchasing teacher and student workbooks and concrete manipulatives kits.

## Math motivation hub

Explore downloadable, free math resources like digital lesson trackers, posters, Mission certificates, and more to inspire math learning in your classroom and schoolwide.

## Family support

Looking for ways to encourage success with Zearn Math for your child? We offer free math resources for families in English and Spanish.

## Efficacy research

Students who learn with Zearn experience two times the growth on state assessments. Explore efficacy research.

## Zearn Summer Math Series

Explore targeted digital math lessons designed to help rising 1st- through 9th-graders strengthen core math skills and prepare for the upcoming school year's grade-level learning.

## Case studies

Discover how educators across the country are using Zearn to support their students’ love of learning math.

## Designed for acceleration

Differentiated support is built into grade-level learning. Because we've amassed the largest dataset on K-8 math learning, we can pinpoint the specific content students need to both catch up and move forward in their grade-level learning.
