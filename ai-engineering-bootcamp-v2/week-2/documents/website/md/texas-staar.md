## Frequently asked questions

### How does Zearn support student success on the STAAR math test?

Zearn Math for Texas embeds STAAR-style item formats directly into daily digital lessons. Students regularly interact with item types used on the redesigned STAAR test—including graphing, number lines, drag-and-drop, and fraction models.

### What are the new question types on the STAAR test?

The Texas Education Agency introduced non–multiple-choice questions as part of the STAAR redesign. These include interactive question formats like drag-and-drop, graphing, equation editors, in-line choice, and more. Zearn Math for Texas embeds these STAAR-style question item formats directly into daily digital lessons.

### Is Zearn aligned to the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS)?

Yes. Zearn Math for Texas is built specifically for Texas classrooms to align to the TEKS. Zearn digital content includes terminology, definitions, and models that reflect Texas math standards, ensuring students encounter the same language across classroom instruction and digital learning, all while providing exposure to instructional terms used on the STAAR assessment.

### Does Zearn align to Bluebonnet Learning K-5 Math?

Zearn Math for Texas includes 700+ daily independent digital lessons that follow the scope and sequence of Bluebonnet Learning K-5 Math, giving students daily opportunities to explore the grade-level concepts they are learning with their teacher and peers.
