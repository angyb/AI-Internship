## Frequently asked questions

### Is Zearn Math for Texas approved by the Texas State Board of Education (SBOE)?

Zearn Math for Texas was reviewed through the Instructional Materials Review and Approval (IMRA) process and is SBOE-approved. Texas teacher reviewers awarded it one of the highest Quality scores for K-5 supplemental math materials, including a perfect Quality score for “Balance of Conceptual and Procedural Understanding,” recognizing how our lessons integrate both dimensions of mathematical learning in every grade.  As an SBOE-approved program, Zearn Math for Texas is designated as a high-quality instructional material, meaning districts can apply a state-funded annual entitlement of $40 per student toward purchase and adoption. Districts may also use their ongoing Instructional Materials and Technology Allotment (IMTA) entitlement to fund Zearn Math for Texas.

### How can teachers use Zearn Math for Texas alongside Bluebonnet Learning?

Zearn Math for Texas complements the Bluebonnet Learning K-5 Math curriculum with daily digital lessons that give students opportunities to engage with grade-level math concepts, refine their thinking, and receive support when they struggle. Teachers should start students at the beginning of the Zearn Math for Texas Mission that corresponds to their current Bluebonnet Learning K-5 Math module. Once students are assigned to a Mission, they work through it at their own pace — no daily adjustments needed.

### What if my district is not using Bluebonnet Learning K-5 Math?

Zearn Math for Texas is built specifically for Texas classrooms to align to the TEKS and prepare students for success on STAAR. Students using any TEKS-aligned curriculum will benefit from Zearn’s coherent progression and real-time adaptive support.

### How many lessons should students complete each week?

Research shows that students who consistently complete three or more grade-level Zearn Math digital lessons each week experience significant gains in STAAR math scores. To maximize impact, set a weekly usage goal of three or more grade-level digital lessons.

### Is Zearn Math for Texas aligned to STAAR math?

Zearn Math for Texas gives students structured exposure to the question types and formats they will encounter on the STAAR math assessment by embedding STAAR-aligned practice into daily digital learning. As students work through digital lessons, they solve problems using interactive digital models that mirror question types from the STAAR redesign, including drag-and-drop, fraction models, equation editors, inline choice, and graphing. In addition, Zearn provides teachers with over 120 paper-based STAAR-style practice problems per grade to support flexible, targeted review ahead of the state assessment.

### How do I access Zearn?

As a nonprofit, Zearn provides teachers with free access to our adaptive digital lessons for one classroom of up to 35 students. Schools and districts can purchase paid accounts that offer premium features and content to support implementation of Zearn at scale.

### Does Zearn Math for Texas align with Bluebonnet Learning Secondary Mathematics (Grades 6–8 and Algebra I)?

Zearn Math for Texas grade K-5 digital lessons automatically follow the Bluebonnet Learning K–5 Math scope and sequence, reflecting the curriculum’s language, models, and depth of TEKS coverage, so students have daily opportunities to engage with the same concepts they are learning in class. For grades 6–8, Zearn provides flexible instructional support. Teachers can assign students to the Mission that best supports the unit they are teaching. Each Mission offers a coherent sequence of digital lessons that helps students independently build deep understanding of TEKS-aligned, grade-level concepts. This flexibility allows students to work through content at their own pace without requiring daily lesson assignments.
