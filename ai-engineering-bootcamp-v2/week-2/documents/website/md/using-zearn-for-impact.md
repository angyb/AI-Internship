## Proven impact with strong implementation

Independent research from the Center for Research & Reform in Education at Johns Hopkins University shows students in districts that used Zearn with dedicated implementation support significantly outperformed peers on the state assessment — with strongest gains for students who completed at least 90 grade-level digital lessons over the course of the school year.

## Actionable insights to power grade-level success

Zearn’s suite of in-app reporting equips district leaders, school leaders, and educators with the visibility they need to lead strong implementation, sustain research-backed usage, and celebrate progress with Zearn Math.

### Teacher reports

#### See exactly when students need support

Built-in alerts ensure teachers know when — and where — students are struggling with grade-level math content, so they can plan targeted support connected to what they’re teaching in class. New for the 2026-27 school year, the LiveView report gives teachers visibility into students’ real-time activity on Zearn, so they can monitor progress, encourage focus and persistence, and step in when needed.

#### Track students’ progress through connected grade-level math content

Streamlined dashboards help teachers lead structured Zearn time to reinforce and deepen core instruction. At-a-glance insights help teachers set implementation goals, celebrate learning milestones, and monitor students’ progress through grade-level content connected to what they’re teaching in class.

### Leader reports

#### Monitor and celebrate systemwide implementation

On-demand insights into school- and district-wide usage empower leaders to observe trends, celebrate successes, and make data-driven decisions to drive impact with Zearn Math.

#### Download custom data to dive deeper

The Exports Center enables district and school administrators to download data for analysis at the district, school, grade, and student levels.

## Frequently asked questions

Does Zearn Math work?

Zearn Math is a K-8 math learning platform that earned the highest rating from Evidence for ESSA, the nationally recognized independent clearinghouse, with multiple independent studies showing the program has a statistically significant, causal impact on elementary and middle school student outcomes.  Read the review →

How many Zearn Math lessons should students complete each week?

Zearn Math is designed to be used during structured time as one, coherent element of Tier 1 math instruction to reinforce and deepen the learning elementary and middle school students are doing with their teacher. Research shows students learn most with Zearn Math when they complete 3 lessons per week, or approximately 90 minutes, as a coherent support to classroom instruction.  Explore the research →

Does Zearn offer training/professional development?

Yes, Zearn offers comprehensive training for educators and leaders at every stage of their implementation to help K-8 schools and districts scale impact with Zearn Math. In addition to live, dedicated professional learning sessions delivered virtually or in person by the Zearn team, Zearn offers on-demand curriculum study PD for every concept of K-5 math.  Explore full PD offerings →

Which grade levels is Zearn Math designed for?

Zearn Math is a K–8 math learning platform designed for both elementary school and middle school students. Schools and districts use Zearn alongside grade-level instruction to reinforce learning, monitor progress, and drive measurable math achievement gains.
