## How Zearn Math works

Your child uses Zearn Math as a structured part of their weekly math learning. Teachers assign students Zearn Math lessons that reinforce the concepts they’re learning in class, giving students another chance to deeply explore math ideas, get just-in-time support, and take on new challenges. Every lesson includes a blend of digital learning and handwritten problem solving.

## Frequently asked questions

How do teachers use Zearn Math?

Teachers use Zearn Math to help students build deep understanding and confidence with the grade-level math concepts they are learning in class. Teachers assign lessons connected to classroom instruction. As students learn, teachers can see progress in real time, identify where students may need extra support, and step in to help when needed.

What happens during a Zearn Math lesson?

During a Zearn Math lesson, students explore the same grade-level math concepts they’re learning with their teacher in a structured sequence of activities that blends research-backed digital learning with handwritten problem solving. As they work through each lesson, students

* Explore math concepts through guided activities with on-screen teachers
* Solve math problems both online and by hand, with paper-and-pencil activities in every lesson
* Get built-in support to work through challenges and build confidence in math
* Show what they’ve learned in a mastery quiz.

As students are learning, teachers can monitor their progress to make sure they’re on track and step in if they need additional support.

What research supports Zearn Math?

Zearn Math is backed by strong independent research and has been proven to help students make greater gains in math. Studies of Zearn Math include a large-scale randomized control trial that found positive impacts on student math achievement.

Zearn’s strong evidence base earned the program the highest rating from Evidence for ESSA, the nationally recognized independent clearinghouse that evaluates education research to help schools, districts, and states select evidence-based programs.

Does Zearn Math use student-facing generative AI?

No. Zearn Math does not include student-facing generative AI, chatbot-style interactions, or AI-generated responses in lessons. Students work through structured, grade-level lessons with active problem solving and written work. Zearn does not share personal student data with AI models.

How does Zearn protect student privacy?

Zearn Math is designed for learning, not for advertising, tracking, or data monetization. As a nonprofit, Zearn is committed to safeguarding children’s online privacy.

* No advertising. Zearn does not show ads to students or use student information for advertising.
* No selling or sharing with advertisers. Zearn does not sell student data or share student information with advertisers.
* No student profiling or off-platform tracking. Zearn does not create digital profiles of students and does not use tracking technologies to monitor students across other websites or services.
* No long-term data retention. Zearn retains personal student information only as long as needed to support learning and meet school or district data privacy agreements. Data for inactive accounts is regularly anonymized or deleted.
