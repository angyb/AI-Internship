HOW ZEARN math WORKS

# Built on brain science. Designed for mastery.

Zearn Math is made by teachers to reinforce and deepen teacher-led instruction. Our math lessons are grounded in the leading research on math learning to give students more opportunities to explore, persevere and break through in the grade-level concepts they are learning in class.

## Fluency and conceptual understanding in every lesson

Research is clear: students need both automaticity with math facts and deep understanding of concepts to be successful in math. Zearn Math incorporates this balance every time a student logs in.

## Built on the concrete- pictorial-abstract approach

Zearn Math is designed to reflect the science of how kids learn — moving from concrete experiences to visual models to abstract reasoning — in order to build mastery that lasts.

## Lessons adapt to help students catch up and move forward on grade level

When students struggle, digital lessons adapt just in time with the support and scaffolding students need to work through challenges — without sacrificing grade-level rigor.

## Handwritten work is part of Zearn Math lessons.

Zearn Math lessons integrate paper-and-pencil problem solving, giving students opportunities to reflect on new concepts, demonstrate their thinking, and transfer their learning beyond the screen.

## Technology that complements teaching

Zearn Math is designed for structured use as a part of classroom instruction. Teachers assign students to content connected to the grade-level concepts they’re teaching, and students work at their own pace through a guided sequence of lessons that blends digital learning with off-screen problem solving to deepen understanding and reinforce learning.

## Backed by proven results Backed by proven results

A large-scale, randomized control trial of over 10,000 students found causal evidence of positive, statistically significant impacts of Zearn Math on student math achievement — earning Zearn Math a “Strong” (Tier 1) rating from Evidence for ESSA. Zearn represents a small subset of supplemental math programs with rigorous, third-party validation meeting this bar of evidence.

## Tab: TEACHERS

## How Zearn Math works for teachers: A companion for grade-level learning How Zearn Math works for teachers: A companion for grade-level learning

Zearn Math is designed to complement your daily instruction alongside your curriculum, with research-backed digital lessons that reinforce your teaching, real-time alerts when students are struggling and actionable insights to help students stay on track with grade-level content.

#### Assign high-quality digital content connected to your instruction

Over 1,000 digital lessons are organized into units, called Missions, covering every major concept of K-8 math. Teachers assign the Mission aligned to what they’re teaching, and students work through a sequence of lessons designed to strengthen understanding — with just-in-time scaffolding and support.

#### See when students are on track — and where they need support

Teachers have real-time visibility into students’ progress through grade-level content, making it easy to identify where students need additional support and plan targeted instruction. Zearn Math reports provide an in-the-moment look into students’ activity on Zearn, so teachers can monitor focus, encourage persistence, and step in when students are stuck.

#### Reinforce and extend learning with printable resources

Zearn Math includes printable supplemental resources that extend learning beyond the screen. Teachers can access Student Notes to accompany digital lessons, practice problems to reinforce math concepts, and other instructional materials to deliver targeted support.
