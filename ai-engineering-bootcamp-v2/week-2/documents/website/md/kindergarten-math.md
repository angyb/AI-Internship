## Kindergarten is an opportunity to give every student a strong start to elementary- and middle-school math learning.

Beyond picking up important skills like counting and writing numerals, kindergarten students deeply learn big math ideas that serve as the foundation upon which all future grades build.

At Zearn, we’ve heard from teachers and leaders around the country who tell us that, particularly in the past few years, more students are entering kindergarten at a wider range of starting points than ever before. Educators have expressed the need for more instructional support in order to ensure that every student can have the opportunity for a strong early start to math learning.

As a nonprofit solely focused on K-8 math, Zearn has invested in creating enhanced digital math lessons to ensure every student can build deep understanding of math concepts that will carry them through eighth grade. Each lesson covers the big math ideas that students are learning with their teacher, with built-in supports to ensure every student can engage independently.

Log in or sign up to access Zearn’s enhanced kindergarten digital lessons — free for teachers, always.

Zearn is the 501(c)(3) nonprofit educational organization behind Zearn Math, the top-rated K-8 math learning platform. Rated “Strong” (Tier 1) by Evidence for ESSA, Zearn Math is used by 1 in 4 elementary-school students and more than 1 million middle-school students nationwide. Everything we do is driven by the belief that every kid is a math kid.
