# Louisiana Dept. of Education Review of Zearn Math

## Zearn’s elementary math curriculum, Zearn Math, received a top rating from the Louisiana Department of Education. Louisiana educators rated it a Tier 1 curricular resource that “exemplifies quality” across coherence, rigor, and focus.

After an in-depth review of all curricular materials—including comprehensive materials for teachers to lead daily instruction and software-based lessons where students learn concepts connected to their teacher's instruction—the Louisiana educator review team determined Zearn Math to be a Tier 1 curricular resource. The review team highlighted with Zearn Math, students learn in flexible and feedback-rich environments and are supported in accessing grade-level math with on-ramps and personalized feedback embedded throughout the curriculum.
