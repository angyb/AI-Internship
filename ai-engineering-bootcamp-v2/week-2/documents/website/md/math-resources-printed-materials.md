# Materials & Manipulatives

### Zearn Math Student Edition

$20 per student

Consumable student curriculum workbook, including application and practice problems, templates, and exit tickets for students to use alongside instruction, as well as Student Notes for Independent Digital Lessons. 3-4 volumes, including the Zearn Math Student Notebook.

### Zearn Math Student Notebooks

$12 per student

Includes all student-facing materials needed for using Zearn Math digital lessons. Single-volume Zearn Math Notebooks include Student Notes for Independent Digital Lessons with adhesive binding and perforated pages. Also available in Spanish for grades K-5.

### Zearn Math Teacher Edition

$110 per teacher

Zearn Math Teacher Editions are available for Grades K-8 and include the following instructional materials:

Course Guide ‍Educative supports teachers need to accurately address grade-level expectations of the standards.

Mission Books Unit-level offering lesson materials and instructional guidance for leading every day of instruction.

Assessments All mid- and end-of-unit assessments, along with detailed answer guides and rubrics.

### Math Manipulatives Kits

Pricing varies by grade level

Everything classrooms need to conduct rich, hands-on learning. Zearn Math manipulatives kits are available for purchase through Hand2Mind.
