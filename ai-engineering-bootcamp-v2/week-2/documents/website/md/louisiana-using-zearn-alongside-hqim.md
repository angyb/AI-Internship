New for the 2026-27 school year, embedded Curriculum Companion Guides make it even easier for Louisiana teachers to assign content that coherently connects to the grade-level concepts they are teaching with their high-quality curriculum.

## Frequently asked questions

How does Zearn Math work alongside Eureka Math²?

Zearn Math is designed to reinforce and deepen the grade-level math students are learning with Eureka Math². The Louisiana Curriculum Companion Guide for Eureka Math², available directly in the Zearn Math platform, helps teachers identify and assign the Zearn Math Mission connected to their current module—without guesswork or extra planning. Each Mission includes a sequence of digital lessons that builds deep understanding through the Concrete-Representational-Abstract progression, provides adaptive support when students need it, and allows students to move ahead when concepts are clicking. Teachers assign at the Mission level, so students can work at their own pace while staying focused on the same grade-level math they are learning in class.

How do teachers assign content in Zearn Math?

Louisiana teachers can use the Curriculum Companion Guide for their district’s approved high-quality instructional material to assign Zearn Math content connected to the unit they are teaching. Teachers can access guides from their Zearn Math Welcome Page, find the curriculum unit they are beginning, and assign the connected Zearn Math Mission with just a few clicks. Students then work through the Mission in order—no daily lesson assignment is required. At the start of each new curriculum unit, teachers return to the guide to review the recommended Mission. In some cases, students will continue working in the same Mission; in others, teachers will assign a new one. Because assignments happen at the Mission level, students can work at their own pace while staying focused on the same grade-level math they are learning in class.

How do I access my Louisiana Curriculum Companion Guide?

Louisiana educators can access the Curriculum Companion Guide for their district’s approved high-quality instructional material from the Zearn Math Welcome Page. Log in to the Zearn Math platform, open the guide for your curriculum, and pin it for easy access. Curriculum Companion Guides are currently available directly in the Zearn Math platform only for educators in Louisiana and South Carolina. They are not currently available within Zearn Math accounts nationwide. Educators nationwide can find Curriculum Companion Guides in the Zearn Help Center.

Are Curriculum Companion Guides available in the Zearn Math platform nationwide?

No. Curriculum Companion Guides are currently available directly in the Zearn Math platform only for educators in Louisiana and South Carolina. They are not currently available within Zearn Math accounts nationwide. Educators nationwide can find Curriculum Companion Guides in the Zearn Help Center and use them to identify the Zearn Math Missions that align with units in their core math curriculum.
