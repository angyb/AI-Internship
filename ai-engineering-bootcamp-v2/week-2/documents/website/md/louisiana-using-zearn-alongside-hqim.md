## Frequently asked questions

How does Zearn Math work alongside Eureka Math2?

Zearn Math is designed to reinforce and deepen grade-level instruction with Eureka Math2 . The new in-app Companion Guide for Eureka Math2 makes it easy for teachers to identify and assign the Zearn Math Mission connected to their current Eureka Math2 module — without guesswork or extra planning. Each Mission includes a sequence of digital lessons that helps students build deep understanding of grade-level math concepts through the Concrete-Representational-Abstract progression, with adaptive support when they need it and opportunities to move ahead when concepts are clicking. Because teachers assign at the Mission level, students can work at their own pace while remaining focused on the same grade-level math they are learning in class.

How do teachers assign content in Zearn Math?

Teachers can use new, embedded Curriculum Companion Guides in Zearn’s platform to assign content connected to the curriculum unit they are teaching in class. Once a guide is pinned, teachers can access it directly from their Zearn Welcome Page, find the curriculum unit they are starting, and assign the connected Zearn Math Mission with just a few clicks. Students then work through the Mission in order — no daily lesson assignment required. At the start of each new curriculum unit, teachers return to the guide to check the recommended Mission. In some cases, students will continue in the same Mission; in others, teachers will assign a new one. Because assignments happen at the Mission level, students can work at their own pace while staying focused on the same grade-level math they are learning in class.

How do I access my Curriculum Companion Guide?

Log in to Zearn Math to find the Companion Guide for your districts’ high-quality curriculum directly from your Zearn Math Welcome Page.
