## Frequently asked questions

### Is Zearn Math for Texas SBOE approved?

Zearn Math for Texas is approved by the Texas State Board of Education (SBOE). Through the Instructional Materials Review and Approval (IMRA) process, Texas teacher reviewers awarded Zearn Math for Texas one of the highest Quality scores (93%) among K–5 supplemental math programs.

Reviewers also awarded Zearn a perfect Quality score for “Balance of Conceptual and Procedural Understanding,” recognizing how every Zearn Math for Texas lesson ensures students build both procedural fluency and conceptual understanding in alignment with the Texas Research-Based Instructional Strategies (RBIS) for math.

Built specifically for Texas classrooms, the program includes over 700 digital lessons across 40 instructional units that follow the Bluebonnet Learning scope and sequence to reinforce and deepen classroom instruction. For districts & schools adopting in the 26-27 academic year, all K-5 digital lessons will be available in both English and Spanish.

Grounded in the Concrete–Representational–Abstract (CRA) progression and aligned to the Texas Research-Based Instructional Strategies (RBIS) for math, lessons reflect the language, models, and learning objectives of Bluebonnet Learning, ensuring a coherent experience across classroom and independent digital learning.

Embedded problem solving blends paper-and-pencil activities that help students strengthen and transfer their learning with STAAR-style digital item types that give students exposure to the question formats they will encounter on the state assessment. When students struggle, Zearn’s adaptive platform delivers targeted, real-time support, helping them catch up while maintaining grade-level rigor.

As an SBOE-approved program, Zearn Math for Texas is designated as a high-quality instructional material, meaning districts can apply a state-funded annual entitlement of $40 per student toward purchase and adoption. Districts may also use their ongoing Instructional Materials and Technology Allotment (IMTA) entitlement to fund Zearn Math for Texas.

### Is Zearn aligned to Bluebonnet K-5 Math?

Zearn Math for Texas is designed to be a daily, Tier 1 support for Bluebonnet Learning K-5 Math. Zearn’s progression of digital lessons automatically follows the Bluebonnet Learning K-5 Math scope and sequence, reinforcing TEKS-aligned instruction and giving students daily opportunities to explore the grade-level concepts they are learning with their teacher.

### How does Zearn support student success on the STAAR math test?

Zearn Math for Texas embeds STAAR aligned practice into daily digital lessons, giving students structured exposure to the question types and formats they will encounter on the STAAR math assessment. As students work independently through digital lessons, they solve problems using interactive digital models that mirror question types from the STAAR redesign, including drag-and-drop, fraction models, equation editors, inline choice, and graphing. In addition, Zearn provides teachers with over 120 paper-based STAAR-style practice problems per grade to support flexible, targeted review ahead of the state assessment.

### Is Zearn aligned to TEKS?

Yes. Zearn Math for Texas is built specifically for Texas classrooms to align to the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) — and is approved by the Texas State Board of Education as a high-quality instructional material. Zearn digital content includes terminology, definitions, and models that reflect Texas standards, ensuring students encounter the same language across classroom instruction and digital learning, all while providing exposure to instructional terms used on the STAAR assessment.

### Is Zearn Math supported by evidence-based research in Texas?

Zearn Math has received a “Strong” (Tier 1) rating from Evidence for ESSA — the highest designation from a nationally recognized, independent clearinghouse that evaluates education research to help schools, districts, and states select proven programs. Read the review. This Tier 1 rating is based on a large-scale randomized controlled trial by RAND (2022–2024). The study, conducted across 10,000 students in grades 3–5 in 64 schools in a large urban Texas district, found causal evidence of positive, statistically significant impacts of Zearn on student math achievement, as measured by the nationally normed NWEA MAP Growth assessment.
