# This one's for you, Teachers!

In celebration of YOU, and with gratitude, we’ve made these downloadable reminders. Grab the ones that you need—or that make you smile. And feel free to share them with friends and fellow teachers, too.

DesktopsWatches & PhonesPrintables

## Desktop wallpapers

Sized just right for your PC or Mac. After you download the image, you can use your display settings to apply your new wallpaper.

## Watch faces

Just-in-time motivations and helpful reminders. Save your favorites to your phone, and then use the Watch app to change your watch face. Select Face Gallery, then Photos to create a new face.

## Phone wallpapers

Pocket-sized inspiration! Grab your favorite (or favorites) and use the settings menu in your phone to personalize your wallpaper. Use the Watch app to change your watch face. Select Face Gallery, then Photos to create a new face.
