# Curriculum reviews

## Zearn Math Named to Tennessee-Approved List

Tennessee teacher reviewers selected Zearn Math for Tennessee for inclusion in the Official List of Textbooks and Instructional Materials for mathematics. Zearn joins the list as the only nonprofit curriculum publisher that is solely dedicated to K–8 math learning.

## Utah State Board of Education Review of Zearn Math

The Utah State Board of Education recommended Zearn Math for all grades K–5 in Utah, labeling it as a "Recommended Primary" curriculum—the highest possible distinction. The Zearn Math curriculum aligns with Utah state standards and meets all high-quality indicators, including focus, coherence, rigor, and balance.

## Louisiana Dept. of Education Review of Zearn Math

After an in-depth review of all Zearn Math’s curricular materials—including comprehensive teaching materials and digital lessons for students—the Louisiana educator review team determined Zearn Math to be a Tier 1 curricular resource. Louisiana educators noted Zearn Math “exemplifies quality” across coherence, rigor, and focus.

## New Mexico Public Education Dept. Review of Zearn Math

Following a rigorous review process by New Mexico’s Public Education Department, Zearn Math received a score of 95% or higher for grades K-5. Teacher reviewers recommended Zearn Math’s elementary math curriculum for all grades K-5 in New Mexico.

## EdReports Review of Zearn Math

Zearn Math received a green rating across all three review gateways: Focus & Coherence, Rigor & Mathematical Practices, and Usability.
