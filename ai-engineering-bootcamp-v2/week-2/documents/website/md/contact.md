# Let’s be in touch!

### Partnerships

We partner with schools, districts, and professional learning organizations to support implementation of Zearn Math. To learn more, complete the form below and our Partnerships Team will be in touch.

### Support

Our Help Center includes step-by-step guides, video tutorials, and troubleshooting tips. For technical support, please visit our Help Center or email our team at support@zearn.org. Due to the volume of requests, we prioritize questions from our School Account partners.

### Careers

In 2012 we founded Zearn because we believe that universal access to an excellent education can change the world. We want to work with others who believe this, too. Visit our Careers page for current openings.

### Donate

We are committed to ensuring all children have access to a high-quality math education. We rely on your generous support to ensure Zearn Math is and remains the most rigorous, supportive, and engaging math teaching and learning experience available for all kids.

### Press inquiries

To get in touch with the communication team at Zearn, please email pressinquiry@zearn.org

### Feedback

We are so grateful to the thousands of teachers across the country whose guidance helps us continuously improve Zearn Math. To submit feedback to our team, contact us at support@zearn.org For any other inquiries, drop us a line at the address below: Zearn PO Box 24580 New York, NY 10087
