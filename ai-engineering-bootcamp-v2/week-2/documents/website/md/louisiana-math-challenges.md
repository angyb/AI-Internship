## How do challenges work? How do challenges work? How do challenges work? How do challenges work?

Boost motivation with a one-month challenge! Louisiana Math Challenges are ready-to-use, printable kits that help schools strengthen routines for completing Zearn Math lessons while celebrating student progress schoolwide. Research shows students see the strongest outcomes when they consistently complete three grade-level Zearn Math lessons each week, building toward 90 lessons over the school year.

### Download kits Download kits Download kits Download kits

Each kit includes schoolwide bulletin board materials, lesson trackers, informational flyers, and celebration certificates.

### Track progress Track progress Track progress Track progress

Every time a student completes a Zearn Math lesson, they color in a section of their individual lesson tracker, and as the challenge progresses, the schoolwide bulletin board gets updated.

### Celebrate Celebrate Celebrate Celebrate

Once the challenge is complete, take a moment to celebrate your students' success with printable certificates!
