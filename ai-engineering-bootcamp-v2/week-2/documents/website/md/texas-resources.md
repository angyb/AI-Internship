# Make the most of Zearn Math for Texas

Zearn’s TEKS-aligned digital lessons are designed specifically for Texas classrooms to help all students succeed in grade-level math. Explore resources for educators and families to support students’ math learning with Zearn.

Now SBOE-Approved

## Zearn Math for Texas is IMRA-reviewed and approved by the Texas State Board of Education

Texas teacher reviewers awarded Zearn Math for Texas one of the highest overall Quality scores among K–5 supplemental math materials, including a perfect score for “Balance of Conceptual and Procedural Understanding.”
