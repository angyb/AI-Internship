# Locked student lessons

When a student logs into their Zearn account, they see their personal Student Feed.

The “Next Up” section of the feed is where students see their current grade-level lessons and assignments from their teacher.

The “Badges” section of the feed is where students can go to see Wild Cards,bonuses, and badges they have earned for completing Independent Digital Lessons. Badges for future digital lessons will appear locked until all activities — fluency, guided instruction, and independent practice — in the current digital lesson have been completed.
