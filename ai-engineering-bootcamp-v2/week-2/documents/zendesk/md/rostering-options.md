# Rostering options

With the purchase of a Zearn Math School Account, schools receive access to Zearn rostering services. Zearn supports three methods for setting up your roster.

* One time roster upload: With this option, districts receive a Zearn Spreadsheet Rostering Template or may submit a OneRoster file. Once submitted, administrator, teacher, and student accounts can be managed directly in Zearn.

* Clever: Zearn supports rostering and single sign on through Clever. Once rostering is established, all changes to staff, students, or classes must be made in your SIS or directly in Clever.

* ClassLink: Zearn supports rostering and single sign on through ClassLink. Once rostering is established, all changes to staff, students, or classes must be made in your SIS or directly in your ClassLink Roster Server.
