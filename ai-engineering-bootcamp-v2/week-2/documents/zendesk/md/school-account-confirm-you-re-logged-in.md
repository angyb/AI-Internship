# School Account: Confirm you're logged in

Teachers and administrators whose school has purchased aZearn Math School Account can easily make sure that they are logged into an account associated with their School Account by checking the top right corner of the screen.

Educators who are logged into the account associated with their School Account will see School Account displayed in green, next to their name.
