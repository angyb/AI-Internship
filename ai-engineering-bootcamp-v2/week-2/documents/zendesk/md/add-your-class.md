# Add your class

Our free Individual Accounts are designed for single classrooms with 1 teacher and up to 35 students.

Once you've created an Individual Account, you can follow the steps below to easily add a class:

1. Click Roster at the top of your Zearn homepage.
2. Click Add Class on the left-hand side of the page.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions.

Now that your class is set up, it's time to add your students.

Note: Teachers whose school has purchased a Zearn Math School Account have no limit on classroom size or the number of classrooms they can add.
