# Anchor charts and posters

Anchor charts and classroom visuals are great ways to help motivate your students.

Teachers can use anchor charts to track student progress, encourage perseverance, prompt discussion, and remind students that making mistakes is how we learn— especially in math. Download free motivational anchor charts and friendly reminders to use in your classroom.
