# Wild Cards

Wild Card videos are designed to be rewarding moments of joy for students to discover as they work through their Independent Digital Lessons. These videos are included throughout Zearn Math’s digital platform to encourage and reward students as they complete digital lessons. Wild Cards include short videos such as Math Laughs and songs by our Zearn teachers. We periodically release new Wild Cards throughout the year, and these will appear in your Student's Feed. Students can view all of the Wild Cards they have earned by clicking “Badges” on their student home page.
