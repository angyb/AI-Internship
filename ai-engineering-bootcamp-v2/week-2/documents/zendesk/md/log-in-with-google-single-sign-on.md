# Log in with Google single sign-on

Google single sign-on (SSO) allows students who have a Google email address to quickly and easily access their Zearn account. If you'd like the students in your class to use Google SSO, you can edit their usernames to their Google email addresses following the steps below:

1. Click Roster at the top of your Zearn homepage, to see your class.
2. Click on the student's name.
3. Choose Edit in the Student Info section and then change the student’s username to their Google email address in the pop-up.
4. Click Save.

Once your students' usernames have been updated, they will be able to log into their Zearn account by clicking the Sign in with Google link on the Zearn homepage, selecting their Google account, and entering their Google account password.
