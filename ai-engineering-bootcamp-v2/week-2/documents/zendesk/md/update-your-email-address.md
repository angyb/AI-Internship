# Update your email address

You can update the email address associated with your Zearn account at any time by following the steps below:

1. Log in to Zearn with your current username/email and password.
2. Click on your name in the top right corner. Select My Account.
3. Select Edit from the Info section on the left.
4. Type in your new email address.
5. Click Save.
