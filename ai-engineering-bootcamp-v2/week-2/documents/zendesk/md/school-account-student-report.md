# School Account: Student Report

The Student Report is available as part of a Zearn Math School Account. As students learn with grade-level digital lessons aligned to core instruction, the Student Report provides teachers with visibility into precisely which topics a student may be excelling in, which topics a student may be struggling with, and how deep the struggle is.

The information in the Student Report empowers teachers to assess struggle side-by-side with other information—such as productivity of the struggle, where in the scope and sequence struggle is occurring, and other formative assessment data—so that teachers are empowered with all of the information they need to differentiate instruction and support individual students.

Teachers who are a part of a School Account can view the Student Report following these steps:

1. Log in to your Teacher Account.
2. Click on the Reports tab.
3. Within the Class Reports section, select Student Reports.
4. Choose a student to view their report.
