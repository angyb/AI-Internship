# Orient to the Zearn Math digital platform

With access to Zearn’s digital platform, teachers can:

* Explore 1,000+ digital lessons covering every objective of K-8 math, with on-screen teachers, interactive visual models, and built-in Tier 1 intervention
* View real-time reports on student progress and struggle
* Access additional instructional materials to help educators differentiate instruction for students who need more support, as well as provide enrichment opportunities
