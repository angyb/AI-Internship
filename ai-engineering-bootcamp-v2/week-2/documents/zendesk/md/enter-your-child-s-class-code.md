# Enter your child’s class code

Class codes offer an easy way for your child to join their teacher’s classroom. Teachers have access to class codes and should provide students with their specific code. If your child does not have their class code, reach out directly to their math teacher.

Your child doesn’t have to enter a class code every time they log in—just the first time they log in to a specific teacher’s classroom. If your child is logging into a teacher’s classroom for the first time, they’ll be prompted to enter their teacher’s class code after entering their username and password.

Note: Students who use Clever or Classlink to log into Zearn are automatically enrolled in their teacher's classroom and do not need to enter a Class Code.
