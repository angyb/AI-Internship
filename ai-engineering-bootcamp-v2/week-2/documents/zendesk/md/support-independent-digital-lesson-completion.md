# Support Independent Digital Lesson completion

Zearn Math is the only top-rated program that connects hands-on instruction with immersive digital learning. Here are key resources you can use to support students in completing their Digital Lessons.

* Independent Digital Lessons: Getting Started Checklist for Educators: Share this checklist with teachers for key actions to take when getting started with Independent Digital Lessons.
* Independent Digital Lessons: "Look Fors" Guide: Use this guide for key "look fors" to support digital content completion.
* Independent Digital Lessons: Admin Report Analysis Guide: Use this guide to know what data to look for in Admin Reports and key questions to ask to support implementation.
* New for the 2026-27 school year, the LiveView classroom report gives teachers real-time visibility during Zearn time. This report shows students’ activity on Zearn as they’re learning, so teachers can monitor engagement, encourage persistence, and provide support in the moment.
