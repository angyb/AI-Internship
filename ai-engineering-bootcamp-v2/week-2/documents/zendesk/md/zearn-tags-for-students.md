# Zearn tags for students

To foster a culture of math engagement and achievement, we designed Zearn tags that you can download and print for your students. Many teachers use Zearn tags to recognize students for wearing their thinking caps and working hard, persevering through challenging problems, supporting classmates and more! We’ve seen teachers use tags in lots of different ways, from hanging them on lanyards to attaching them to Student Notes or Exit Tickets.

Show us how you use Zearn tags by sharing a photo of your ‘Zearning it’ celebrations and tagging @zearn on Facebook, @zearned on X , or @zearnmath on Instagram.
