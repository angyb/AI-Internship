# Digital Bonuses

Digital Bonuses are problems that students can work on after they complete an Independent Digital Lesson. They are designed to challenge students and extend their learning. Teachers can orient students to using Digital Bonuses for an additional enrichment opportunity.

Digital Bonuses don’t automatically appear in Next Up; they can be accessed from the Badges page by following the steps below:

1. Click Badges on the student home page.
2. Click on a Lesson Badge. Use the arrows to navigate to previous Lessons, Missions, and grades.
3. In the Lesson pop-up, click the Beat It button.
4. Get started on the Bonus!
