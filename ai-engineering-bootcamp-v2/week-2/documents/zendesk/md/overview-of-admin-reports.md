# Overview of Admin Reports

Admin Reports are available as part of a paid Zearn Math School Account. Administrators can use reports to assess and monitor schoolwide progress through on-or-above grade-level content and productivity during independent work:

* The School Goals Report helps administrators track progress toward completion goals at the school, grade, and classroom level to celebrate goal achievement and share best practices.
* The Completion Tracking Report helps administrators monitor and assess Independent Digital Lesson completion to ensure that all students are on track to complete their grade’s scope and sequence.
* District administrators can use the Admin Snapshot Report to log in to observe usage trends and unlock real-time insights across their implementation, empowering leaders to make data-driven decisions to celebrate successes and support improvements. 
* The Exports Center allows district and school administrators to download custom data for deeper analysis at the district, school, grade, and student levels.
