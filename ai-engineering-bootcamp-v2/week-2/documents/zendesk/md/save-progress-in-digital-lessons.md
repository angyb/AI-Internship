# Save progress in digital lessons

As students complete each component of their Independent Digital Lesson, their progress is automatically saved in Zearn’s platform. Students can begin, pause, and return to the same lesson later.

Note: If you're encountering issues with student work being saved, we recommend speaking with your school's IT administrator to make sure domains associated with Zearn have been added to your school or district's allow list and firewall software installed on district-issued devices is up to date. Not allowing Zearn through your firewall or using out-of-date firewall software can cause issues with lesson saving.
