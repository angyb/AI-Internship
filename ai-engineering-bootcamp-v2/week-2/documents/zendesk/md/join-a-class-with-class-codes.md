# Join a class with class codes

Zearn class codes offer an easy way for your students to join your class and keep their Zearn Badges and progress from previous school years. If your school does not access Zearn through Clever or Classlink, you can find your class code on your class page or by printing out your students’ login cards. To quickly find your class code:

1. Click Roster at the top of your Zearn homepage.
2. If you are part of a Zearn Math School Account, click on the desired class.
3. View the Class Code at the top right corner of the page.

For students not enrolled in a class: Students enter their username and password on the Zearn homepage. Next, they will be taken to a new page and prompted to enter your class code. Entering your class code will place them in your class.

For students already enrolled in a class, students can switch to your class by:

1. Logging in with their current username and password.
2. Clicking on the name of their class.
3. Selecting Yes when asked if they need to switch classes.
4. Entering your class code.

By keeping their Zearn account and switching classes, students will save their Badges and you will get access to their previous Zearn Progress Reports.

Note: Teachers who access Zearn through Clever or ClassLink do not have class codes— their class rosters are automatically populated by their school or district's SIS.
