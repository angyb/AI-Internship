# School Account: Help students log in with Clever

First student login through Clever:

If you and your students are using Zearn with Clever, you’ll access Zearn through the Clever portal. When students first log in, they will automatically join your classroom. This guide shows important steps for linking teacher and student Zearn accounts with Clever.

If students have logged in but are not showing up in your classroom, contact your school's Clever Administrator to make sure that students are in your Clever classroom. Class rosters in Clever come directly from your school district's Student Information System (SIS) which is managed by district administrators.

Helping students log in with Clever:

Students who use Clever to access Zearn will log into Zearn directly through their Clever portal. Clever offers a variety of ways for students to log into their accounts; you can reach out to your Clever district admin for more information on the specific login methods available to your students.

If you are experiencing issues or need help, see the Clever troubleshooting tips below:

* I’m a teacher
* I’m helping a student

If you have any questions about accessing your Zearn account through Clever, you can reach the Clever Support Team through their Help Center.
