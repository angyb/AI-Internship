# Class Snapshot Report

The Class Snapshot Report provides a summary class-level view of implementation progress, helping teachers more easily track usage goals and make decisions to drive student engagement and motivation for math learning.

For users with a School Account, the Class Snapshot Report shows a real-time view of student sign-ins, time students spent on Zearn, and the number of on or above grade-level lessons completed per student over the course of the selected time frame. Teachers can also view the school year total for on or above grade-level lessons per student.

By clicking on a metric, teachers can explore trends, view student-level highlights for highest and lowest performance, and access the full data set for all of their students in the selected class.

This report can help identify groups of students that have not signed into Zearn, that need more time to meet their weekly Independent Digital Lesson completion goal, or are not on track to meet their end of year lesson completion goal, as well as students that have already met their goals.
