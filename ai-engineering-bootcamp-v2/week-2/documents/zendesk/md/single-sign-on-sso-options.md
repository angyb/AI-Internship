# Single sign-on (SSO) options

Individual Accounts can access Zearn’s login via Google single sign-on.

Districts and schools using School Accounts can use a Learning Management Systems (LMS) as well as access Zearn through single-sign on (SSO) integrations. Zearn can be embedded within any LMS system, and Zearn School Accounts offers one-click, single-sign on through Clever Instant Login, Google SSO, and Classlink Launchpad. For example, if a district uses Schoology, Clever, and Zearn together, students can log in to Schoology (LMS) using their school credentials and immediately access their daily assignment from their teacher.
