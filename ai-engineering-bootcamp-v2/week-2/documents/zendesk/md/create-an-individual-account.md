# Create an Individual Account

Individual Accounts are available at no cost and offer access to our top-rated math platform and all Zearn Math instructional materials for up to 35 students and 1 teacher.

Account created? Great! Let's add your class.

Note: For schools and districts, we offer paid accounts that offer access to additional features and data. Visit this page to compare account features and request a quote.
