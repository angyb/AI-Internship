# Assign an Independent Digital Lesson

Students are automatically assigned to the first lesson of the first Mission of their grade and continue to progress automatically through the digital sequence. Teachers can manually update their student’s assignment as needed. Students will access these lessons through the “Next Up” section of their Student Feed, which displays their currently-assigned grade-level digital lesson. Teachers who are part of a Zearn Math School Account can also assign foundational lessons for students to complete through the , which is also accessed through the Student Feed.

Teachers can access and preview all Independent Digital Lessons directly through their Zearn account by navigating to “Curriculum” in the top navigation bar, selecting the appropriate grade and Mission, and then scrolling to access digital lessons within that Mission.
