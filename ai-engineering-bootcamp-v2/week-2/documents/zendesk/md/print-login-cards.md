# Print login cards

You can easily share your students’ login information with students and families using student login cards. To access and print login cards for your class, follow the below steps:

1. Navigate to Roster at the top of your Zearn account.
2. If you have more than one class, choose the desired class from the dropdown menu.
3. Click Get Login Cards.
4. Print from the new tab.
