# Delete your Individual Account

Deleting an account is irreversible and all identifiable data associated with the account will be removed. If you need to delete your Individual Account, you can do this by following the instructions below.

1. Log into your account.
2. Click on your name at the top right corner of the screen.
3. Select My Account.
4. Click on Delete Account, shown in red below your class.
5. Enter your password, as prompted in the pop-up, to confirm your account deletion.
6. Click Delete Now.
