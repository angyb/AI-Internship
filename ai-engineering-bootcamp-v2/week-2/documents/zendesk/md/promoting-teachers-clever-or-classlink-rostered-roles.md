# Promoting Teachers' Clever or Classlink Rostered Roles

If you're a school or district administrator of a Zearn Math School Account rostered through Clever or Classlink, you can make edits to your staffs' roles directly in Zearn. Please Note that promoting a teacher's role will give them access to school-wide Admin Reports.

* Log into your Zearn Math School Account and select the "Roster" tab.
* Click on the teacher's name you would like to promote.
* Select the permission level you would like to grant to the user and select "Change Role"

Once a teacher's role is promoted, they will receive an email about their updated access level, which will include information about setting up 2-factor authentication. Additionally, the role field for the updated user will no longer sync with Clever or Classlink.
