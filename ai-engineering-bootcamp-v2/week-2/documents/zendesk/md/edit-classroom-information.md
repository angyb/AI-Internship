# Edit classroom information

Sometimes your class information may change over the school year. If it does, you can edit your class name by following the steps below:

1. Click Roster at the top of your Zearn homepage.
2. Select your class.
3. Click on the gear next to the class name.
4. On the next page, edit your class name.
5. Click Save.
