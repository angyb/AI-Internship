# Using a Zearn.org Backup Login

Note: This article is for Zearn Math School Account holders who are rostered via Cleveror rostered via Classlink

Zearn Math School Account teachers and administrators who are rostered via Clever or Classlink have the ability to create a Zearn.org backup login, allowing them to log into their accounts directly from the Zearn.org homepage. You can create your Zearn.org backup login by following the steps below:

1. Log into your Zearn account
2. Click on your name at the top right corner of the screen
3. Select My Account
4. Click on Set up Zearn.org sign-in
5. Confirm the email address you’d like to use with your backup login
6. Follow the instructions sent via email to set your Zearn.org password

Once you have created your Zearn.org backup login credentials, you can follow these steps to access your students’ Zearn.org usernames and passwords:

1. Log into your account using your Zearn.org sign-in
2. Click Roster at the top of the page
3. Select your class
4. Click Zearn.org usernames and passwords
