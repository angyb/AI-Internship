# Manage assignments for students in multiple classrooms

Students who are part of a Zearn Math School Account are able to have multiple classroom assignments at the same time.

For students assigned to more than one classroom, it is important to note that they will have only one Independent Digital Lesson assignment across all of their classes. This means that if the student's lesson assignment is changed in one class, their assignment will be updated across all classes that they are assigned to.
