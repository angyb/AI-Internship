# Use Zearn as homework

With Zearn Math, students learn new concepts in two ways during core instructional time: Independent Digital Lessons and Collaborative Concept Exploration. If students in your class are struggling to meet their Independent Digital Lesson completion goals during core instructional time or could use extra practice with a concept, you may consider assigning homework.

Printable paper homework aligned to each Mission is available within Teacher Accounts. Additionally, students can access Independent Digital Lessons from home at any time. If you plan to have students complete digital lessons at home, we recommend making sure they have access to Student Notes and paper Exit Tickets so that they can complete them alongside their Independent Digital Lessons.
