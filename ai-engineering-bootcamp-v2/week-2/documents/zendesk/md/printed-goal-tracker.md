# Printed goal tracker

After students complete the Tower of Power, they use the weekly printed goal tracker to keep track of their progress toward their Independent Digital Lesson completion goals. Printed Lesson Trackers for each Mission are included in Zearn Notebooks or can be downloaded and printed individually from Teacher Accounts following the steps in our Navigation Guide.
