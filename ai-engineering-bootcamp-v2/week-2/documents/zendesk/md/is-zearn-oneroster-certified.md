# Is Zearn OneRoster® certified?

Zearn is certified for OneRoster® 1.2 by 1EdTech Consortium, an international standards organization that develops interoperability standards for education technology.

OneRoster is a widely used data standard that allows school systems to securely share roster information between platforms, including students, teachers, classes, and enrollments.

OneRoster certification verifies that a platform can securely and reliably exchange roster data — such as students, teachers, classes, and enrollments — with school district systems like Student Information Systems (SIS) and Learning Management Systems (LMS).

This certification confirms that Zearn supports the OneRoster 1.2 standard for automated rostering and secure roster data exchange used by districts across the United States. View Zearn’s certification in the 1EdTech TrustEd Apps directory.
