# Managing your ongoing Zearn roster through Clever

Once a district has connected with Zearn through Clever, Zearn will update roster information every morning through an automated sync. Staff members are not able to make any changes to classroom rosters or staff members assigned to classes through the Zearn application.

All roster changes must be made directly in the district's SIS. Keep in mind that grade-level assignments only sync on initial roster creation; all subsequent lesson assignments are managed by teachers in their account.
