# Class Reports

Zearn Math Class Reports and individual Student Reports give educators real-time data on student progress and where students might need extra support. We recommend that teachers check reports at least twice per week.

Class-level reports provide insight into student learning: Progress, Pace, Tower Alerts, and Sprint Alerts.

Teachers whose school has purchased a School Account also have access to:

* The Zearn Student Report. This report dives into exactly which topics a student may be excelling in and which topics a student may be struggling with, with greater depth and precision.
* New for the 2026-27 school year, the LiveView classroom report gives teachers real-time visibility during Zearn time. This report shows students’ activity on Zearn as they’re learning, so teachers can monitor engagement, encourage persistence, and provide support in the moment.
