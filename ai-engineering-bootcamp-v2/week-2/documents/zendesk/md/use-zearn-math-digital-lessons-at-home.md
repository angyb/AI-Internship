# Use Zearn Math digital lessons at home

Does your child use Zearn Math at school?

If you'd like your child to use Zearn Math's digital lessons as a complement to their core math instruction but their school does not use Zearn Math, you can sign up for an Individual Account, create a class and add your child to your class.

The articles below will help you get your child ready to begin using digital lessons at home:

1. Create an Individual Account.
2. Add your class.
3. Add students.
4. Change your student's assignment.
