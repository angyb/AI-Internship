# On-screen keypad

As students work through Zearn's Independent Digital Lessons, they have the option to use an on-screen keypad, rather than a computer keyboard, to type and submit answers. This accessibility feature is particularly important for tablet users and young students who may not yet know how to use a computer keyboard.
