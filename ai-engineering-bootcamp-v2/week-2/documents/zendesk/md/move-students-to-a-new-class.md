# Move students to a new class

If you're part of a Zearn Math School Account and your school does not access Zearn through Clever or Classlink, you can follow the steps below to move your students from one of your classrooms to another:

1. Click Roster at the top of your Zearn homepage.
2. Choose the class that contains the student(s) you’d like to move from the dropdown menu.
3. Click the box next to the name(s) of the student(s).
4. Click Move Students.
5. In the pop-up, choose your students' new classroom and click Move.

Note: If you are part of a Zearn Math School Account that accesses Zearn through Clever or Classlink, you will need to reach out to your Clever or Classlink administrator to update your student's classroom assignments in Zearn.
