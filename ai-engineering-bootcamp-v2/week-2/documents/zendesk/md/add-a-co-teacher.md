# Add a co-teacher

If you are part of a Zearn Math School Account and your school does not access Zearn through Clever or Classlink, you can share your class with a co-teacher by following the steps below:

1. Click Roster at the top of your Zearn homepage.
2. Click on the desired class.
3. On the left side of the screen, click Share Class.
4. Follow the prompts to select a teacher to share your class with, then click Share.

Note: If your school uses Clever or Classlink to manage its roster, co-teachers must be added by the Clever/Classlink SIS administrator at your school.
