# View my child's progress

There are a variety of methods you can use to keep track of your child's progress. One option is to check the Lesson Tracker at the top of your child's Student Feed. This tracker shows the exact lesson your child is assigned to and the specific activity in that lesson that they’re currently working on.

You can also track progress by viewing the lesson badges your child has earned in “Badges.”

For more detailed information on your child’s progress with Zearn Math, we recommend reaching out to your child’s teacher. Teachers have access to multiple reports — including an individual Student Report—that offer progress updates side-by-side with other information to provide a holistic view of your child’s math learning.
