# Contact Zearn support

Zearn supports teachers, students, and families through this Help Center and email support. As a nonprofit, we are not resourced to provide phone support and do not have a phone support line. That said, we update this Help Center frequently to provide answers to commonly asked questions, and we encourage our partners to use this as a resource.

If you can't find the answer you are looking for on our Help Center, you can email us at support@zearn.org. Our team is available 9am–8pm EST, Monday–Friday.
