# Reset your password

If you don't remember the password to your Zearn account, you can easily reset your password by following these steps:

1. Navigate to Zearn.org and click “I forgot my password.”
2. Enter the email address associated with your Teacher Account and click “Send link.”
3. Check your email for a link to reset your Zearn password. If you don't see the email in your inbox, be sure to check your junk, spam, and clutter folders, as sometimes district filters catch the password reset email.
