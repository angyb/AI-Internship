# Enrichment opportunities

If students are demonstrating mastery in assessments and meeting their weekly Independent Digital Lesson completion goals, teachers can provide opportunities for additional challenge and growth.

Zearn Math recommends the following enrichment options that are aligned to students' current grade-level learning with Independent Digital Lessons.

Digital Bonuses:

Digital Bonuses are challenging problems students can work on after they complete an Independent Digital Lesson. Teachers can assign students to work on bonuses to enrich and extend learning. Bonuses do not appear automatically in the Student Feed— teachers should direct students to navigate to their Badges page in their Student Feed to access Bonuses.

Optional Enrichment Problems:

Teacher materials for Concept Exploration highlight extra, above-grade-level problems that teachers can assign to students for enrichment.
