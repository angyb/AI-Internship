# Roster teacher and students to School Accounts

With every purchase of a Zearn Math School Account, your school or district receives complete rostering and deployment support from the Zearn team. Zearn offers a variety of ways to roster staff and students, including via integration with Clever, Classlink or with a school- or district-provided spreadsheet. Upon purchasing a School Account, a deployment specialist will contact your school or district to set up your Zearn Math School Account.

If you have questions or need to make updates to your existing School Account during the school year, please reach out to support@zearn.org.
