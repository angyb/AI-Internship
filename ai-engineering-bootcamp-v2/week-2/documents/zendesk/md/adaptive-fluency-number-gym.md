# Adaptive fluency: Number Gym

Every grade 1-5 Independent Digital Lesson includes Number Gym, an adaptive fluency activity that helps students build foundational number sense. For older students, Number Gym activities are designed to bridge K–5 math foundations, reinforce previously learned skills, and address areas of unfinished learning.

As students demonstrate proficiency in an activity, they progress to larger numbers and more challenging portions of the Number Gym sequence.

Try out each Number Gym activity below:

* Make and Break
* Next Stop Top
* Number Bond Dash
* Take From 10
* Take From 10: Take Two
* Make Ten
* Make Ten Returns
* Form to Form
* The Counting Train
* Hop Skip Splash!
* Sum Snacks
* Bundle the Sea
* Count the Cosmos
* Polar Place Values
