# Zearn Math for Kindergarten

In addition to developing important skills like counting and writing numerals, Kindergarten students learn big ideas, like whole numbers and operations, geometry, and measurement, that serve as the foundation for so much of their future elementary-school math learning.

Featuring 100+ expanded digital lessons, Zearn Math for Kindergarten is designed to help every student build deep understanding of Kindergarten math — on a platform tailored for young learners. Each lesson covers the big math ideas that students are learning with their teacher, with built-in supports to ensure every student can engage independently.
