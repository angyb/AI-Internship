# Share classrooms on Zearn

Teachers whose school has purchased a Zearn Math School Account can share their classrooms with other teachers and administrators.

If you’re part of a Zearn Math School Account and your school does not access Zearn through Clever or Classlink, you can follow the steps below to share your classroom with other staff members at your school:

1. Click Roster at the top of your Zearn Homepage
2. Choose the class you’d like to share from the drop-down menu
3. Click Share Class
4. In the pop-up, choose the grade and then select the Teacher with whom you'd like to share the class
5. Click Share

Note: If your district has integrated Zearn through Clever or Classlink, classroom sharing will be managed directly in your SIS.
