# Using your District’s ClassLink Launchpad

All staff and students must access Zearn through their district’s ClassLink Launchpad or by selecting Log in with ClassLink on the Zearn.org homepage. When a district integrates with Zearn through ClassLink, accounts created outside of ClassLink are not associated with the district’s Zearn Math School Account.

If staff or students notice that progress or classroom data is missing, it is often because they logged in using an account created outside of ClassLink. Logging in through ClassLink ensures that activity is connected to the correct Zearn Math School Account.
