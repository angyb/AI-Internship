# Zearn Math for homeschooling

Zearn Math can be used for homeschooling. Families looking to use Zearn Math in a homeschool setting can create an Individual Account to access our digital lessons, lesson-aligned student materials, teacher materials, and class reports.

For information on how to create and manage your account, visit the Set up Individual Accounts section of the Help Center.

For information on how to use Zearn to complement your instruction and support student learning, visit the Preparing to teach sectionof the Help Center.
