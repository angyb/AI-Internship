# Accessibility

As a nonprofit on a mission to help all students love learning math, we strive to make Zearn Math accessible for all students. We've made intentional decisions meant to support a diverse range of learners throughout the Zearn Math student experience. Our commitment to accessibility is ongoing and we will continue to make updates to ensure our instructional materials are usable for all students, including those with disabilities.
