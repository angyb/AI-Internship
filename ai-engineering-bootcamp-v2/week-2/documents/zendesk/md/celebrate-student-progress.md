# Celebrate student progress

Whether students are meeting weekly goals or persevering through challenging concepts, progress and hard work are worth celebrating! Explore the Math Motivation Hub for downloadable, free math resources like challenges, digital lesson trackers, posters, certificates, and more to inspire students.

Below are a few of the ways we’ve seen teachers motivate students and celebrate learning:

* Decorate student work or mark progress through digital lessons with Zearn-inspired stickers
  * Zearn and emoji sticker sheet
  * Star stickers for completed lessons

* Create a bulletin board to inspire their math learning
  * March Madness bulletin board
  * Motivational math bulletin board(download supplies here)

* Use certificates to highlight progress or success in reaching weekly goals
  * Example use of Zearn Mission Completion Certificate (download grade-level Mission certificates here)
  * Goal-achievement certificates with rainbow colors
  * Brainy-themed Weekly lesson tracker (download the tracker here)

* Host a party to celebrate a week, month, or year full of math learning
  * Zearn-themed pizza party
  * Ice cream party
  * Weekly celebration for those who met their lesson goals

* Mix up math and music and perform a learning-inspired jam
  * My nines, oh oh!
  * Roll your 3s, work it out!
* Access our celebration tags and motivational classroom posters
  * Celebration tags
  * Motivational math posters

Follow @zearn on Facebook, @zearned on X , and @zearnmath on Instagram for more examples of learning celebrations from educators across the country.
